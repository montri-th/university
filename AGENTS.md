# CityMETER University — owner preferences

Set 2026-11-12 (release 0.1.0) on the owner request, VERBATIM: “จากที่เราทำ demo ของ CityMETER: Hotel มาแล้ว ใน https://montri-th.github.io/hotel ขอให้ทำแบบเดียวกันให้กับ CityMETER: University แล้ว publish ที่ https://github.com/montri-th/university โดยใช้ข้อมูลจาก [the university research master] update dataset registry sheet ด้วยนะ และพี่ช่วยสร้าง repo ใหม่ใน github ให้ด้วย”. “แบบเดียวกัน” = the CityMETER · Hotel contract as the School demo carries it after Phase 0 (code base montri-th/school @793da70, citymeter-shell 0.6.0), so every Hotel or School rule that is not about hotels or schools applies here as written. Hotel-only and School-only features (chain logos and room prices, district fills, the comparison set, learning services, closures) have no counterpart here and are not carried.

- Apply current LDS 0.9.7 (v0.9.7-owner.1, standalone-0.9.7-r1, color-srgb-10) and the Location Intelligence Profile for LDS 0.9.7 (`li.supply` for students), and preserve the map-first CityMETER layout. Colours come only from `assets/theme.css`, `js/scales.js` and `js/cues.js`; never approximate a value by eye. Analytical colours are the same HEX on both themes; EVID-05 map treatments use the `dataState` tokens. Colour scales always use the full 41 LDS samples (owner standing rule “Color scale ขอให้ใช้ 41 scales ตาม LDS เสมอ”). `npm run check:lds -- --lds <base> --li <profile>` compares every shipped value with both source files.
- Read `PRODUCT_STATEMENT.md` before changing what the first screen argues. The question is “นักศึกษากระจุกอยู่จังหวัดไหน และรอบแปลงมีวิทยาเขตแบบไหน”.
- Two grains, never mixed (`DATA_METHOD.md` §2). **Reporting units** (the MHESI student file 2568/2: a university, campus or institution the file reports separately) carry the students and the province the file names for them; they are the measure. **Sites** (the research team's reviewed university areas, `29_DEMO_SITES`) carry the points, the extents, the faculties and the context; a site shows its linked unit's figure as that unit's (“นักศึกษาตามหน่วยรายงาน …”), never as the students on that campus. A unit is linked to at most one site and a site names at most one unit; every link says how it was made (`js/model.js#LINKS`). A site without a link shows no student figure — never 0.
- Measure is a contract (`js/model.js#METRICS`): students reported (Σ students of the reporting units, “คน”, `li.supply`) by default, reporting units (“หน่วย”, `count`) on the switch; the map, legend, labels, Aha, ranks and charts read the same measure. Never call a figure an enrolment rate, demand, seats, capacity, a catchment, a market share, a density or a ranking of quality.
- Province is the finest area. Membership is the province the student file names for the unit; never re-assign it by a point or a name at runtime. The 77 production provinces are drawn; a province without a reporting unit is measured zero (outline, no fill) and is listed by name.
- Types are the eight groups of `meta.types` bound by stable id to LDS series with their shape cue (`js/cues.js#GROUP_SERIES`; no star). The two open-admission universities are the `open` type, by id, never by a name.
- Levels come from the file's level columns only: ป.ตรี · ป.โท · ป.เอก · อื่น ๆ (ปวช. ปวส. ประกาศนียบัตรบัณฑิต ประกาศนียบัตรบัณฑิตชั้นสูง ประกาศนียบัตร และอนุปริญญา). Never infer a level from a name.
- Aha first: on the whole country, the provinces with the most of the measure (at most three; never cut through a tie, never every province that holds the measure; `js/area-insight.js#topWithoutTie`), their share of the national total with numerator and denominator, the same share without the open-admission universities as a second bar (both bases in each bar's title and accessible text) and the provinces with no reporting unit by name; in a province, the universities with the most of the measure (at most three) and their share of the province. Snapshot numbers in the handoff JSON are fixtures, never UI constants.
- Fees are per programme as the university announces them (`32_DEMO_OFFERINGS` → `33_DEMO_FEE_LINKS`, the pilot campuses only): every line its own amount in its own currency (`js/model.js#money`, never converted), the research codes in words (`FEE_WORDS`, `cohortWords`; `tests/views.test.js` fails on a code without a word), the announcement linked. Never sum, average or compare fees across programmes or universities, never call one a price level or the cost of a degree, and never read a programme in an announcement as a programme admitting students now (the heading is “ค่าธรรมเนียมตามประกาศ”, the count “หลักสูตรในประกาศ”).
- Quality (`08_QUALITY`) is shown verbatim with its framework, dates and source, never as a rank or a score to compare.
- Logos come only from the research team's package (`LOGO_LIBRARY`), checked against its SHA-256 and shipped content-addressed (`assets/logos/<sha16>.<ext>`); they identify the university, under the name, on a plate of the surface the package names. Reuse terms are not established in the source: keep the note in `DATA_METHOD.md` and never use a logo as decoration.
- Campus extents are OpenStreetMap areas the research team matched (ODbL), approximate: “กรอบราว N ไร่” (m² / 1,600), never a deed, a legal boundary or a land size. An area the university publishes is shown as it is written (`officialArea`): a single figure as a chip, a longer note under the sources.
- Faculty buildings: a faculty's building geometry joins the drawn buildings by its interior point (`pt`) at runtime; the building panel says “สูงราว” and heads its list “คณะในอาคารนี้” — the faculties whose geometry falls in the drawn outline, never “ตั้งอยู่ใน” beyond that.
- Roster points (the MHESI roster 2566) are quiet context only: never counted, never clickable, a record whose point lies in another province than its own is not drawn (`meta.checks.rosterPointsNotDrawn`).
- Context comes from datasets of the same registry, joined by id with the commit and SHA-256 recorded (`meta.context`): CityMETER · Transit stations (operating stations drawn as quiet clickable rings, 1 km round a station; under construction named in words only) and CityMETER Shopping Center venues linked by the research team. Every distance is a straight line between reference points — never a walking route, a travel time or a catchment.
- Focus + quiet context: detail (fills, coloured cue symbols, extents, faculty buildings) only for the focus; everything else stays visible but quiet. The campus file (`data/campus.json`) loads after the first paint (1.2 s) or when a campus or a university is opened; until then the panel says it is loading, never empty.
- Names near the middle of the view (`js/labels.js`): campus labels in the central ellipse, at most 9 on desktop and 5 on a phone; a label is the type cue and a short name (the leading มหาวิทยาลัย/สถาบัน dropped, “(ชื่อพื้นที่จาก OSM)” dropped); hover or focus opens a card of icon chips.
- Terrain and the building view (`js/shell/terrain.js`, `js/shell/camera.js`, `js/massing.js`, `js/shell/venue-xray.js`, `js/shell/maplibre-guard.js`, MapLibre pinned at 5.6.2, `GEOJSON_MAXZOOM`) follow the Hotel and School contracts unchanged. A campus opens in the `model` preset framed on its extent and faculty geometry; a faculty building opens the two-point camera. Do not reorder the massing, x-ray, station, campus and pin layers casually.
- Breadcrumb is ประเทศไทย › จังหวัด › the campus, with production province codes; the campus crumb has no production link and the address writes no `d` (the registry gives this dataset no production code).
- Every panel view ends with exactly one provenance line (`provenance()` in `js/panel.js`): dataset · source · date · boundary as pictogram-led parts, then “วิธีนับ” (DATA_METHOD.md); the roster, OpenStreetMap, the universities' own pages, rail and terrain join the source part when the view shows them. No caveat anywhere on screen (Hotel owner rule of 2026-11-01, “ไม่มี caveats เลย”): exactness comes from the names, the numerator and denominator of every share and the provenance line (`tests/views.test.js`).
- Copy: short, natural Thai in the reader's words — นักศึกษา / หน่วยรายงาน / มหาวิทยาลัย / วิทยาเขต / คณะ / หลักสูตร / ค่าธรรมเนียม / ประเภทสถาบัน / จังหวัด / สถานี / รอบแปลง; pictograms from the approved subset carry what a sentence used to; sources, methods and ids behind one “ที่มาและพิกัด” disclosure; units on every number; a number and its unit never split across lines. No static counts in page copy (help, meta): numbers come from the data.
- English (`?lang=en`, citymeter-shell i18n): `js/lang.js` registers `js/lang-en.js` and the English the data carries (province and district names of the area index, station nameEn, `meta.types[].en`). University, campus, unit and faculty names have no English in the data and stay Thai, and so do programme names and quality texts as published. Years are ค.ศ. in English (“semester 2/2025”). Run a harvest in English (every view, every pilot campus, every province) before a release so no view is left in Thai.
- Outbound actions (`js/ask.js`): ถาม Google AI with a question written for the view, Google Maps search, Street View only where the view has a point. Public names and published points only. Nothing from Google's answers enters the data.
- Phone layout (Hotel 0.3.5, School 0.6.0): below 761 px in portrait the Aha line and the legend are one dock and the sheet has three heights; verify at 390 px in light and dark.
- Privacy and publication: never copy the roster's official institution codes (UNIV_ID), canonical ids or source record numbers as fields, researcher notes, review queues, identity statuses, raw source locators or internal Sheet / Drive URLs (`meta.excludedFields`); source links are public https pages only. Never commit the research sheet, its cells, its export or its address, the dataset registry's address, credentials, the full LDS library or the Location Intelligence Profile (`tests/release.test.js` knows the two sheet addresses only by their SHA-256). Never put Landometer prices, contracts, proposals or pipeline in this repository. Review Thai and English at desktop and 390 px in light and dark before claiming done; local renders are not proof of a live release — run `npm run verify:live` and walk the live site.
- Releases bump one cache tag `?v=YYYYMMDD-rN` on every entry in `index.html` and, through the import map there, on every ES module, together with `package.json` version, `design-intent.json` and the handoff JSON `productVersion` (`tests/release.test.js`); the campus file takes the same tag from `import.meta.url`. A new module must be added to the import map. Icon fonts inside the stylesheets carry `?v=<first 8 hex of their SHA-256>` (`tests/icons.test.js`).
- Registry: the CityMETER Dataset Registry sheet row `research.TH.university` carries the demo columns (web, repository, checked date, check note); edit those cells in place, never a new copy of the sheet.

## Discovery and dataset group

- Every page carries `<meta name="robots" content="noindex, nofollow">` (owner 2026-11-08: “ไม่ต้องการให้เสิช engine เข้ามาดูเดโม แต่อยากให้ dev นำ demo บน github ไป vibe coding ต่อได้ง่าย …”). Keep the rest of the SEO metadata ready for production, do not block crawlers in a robots.txt, add no sitemap, and never point `rel="canonical"` at landometer.com from a noindex page. `tests/discovery-and-group.test.js` guards this.
- The panel eyebrow `CITYMETER · LIVING` follows the dataset registry (`R01_DATASETS.dataset_group` of `research.TH.university`, read 2026-11-12; owner: “ใช้ตามทะเบียนทั้งหมด”). Change the label only when the registry changes.

## Shared shell (citymeter-shell)

- `js/shell/` is citymeter-shell, the same bytes in every CityMETER demo; `js/shell/manifest.json` names the version and the hash of every file (`tests/shell.test.js`). Never edit it in one repository alone.
- This release ships **citymeter-shell 0.6.1**: 0.6.0 plus University in the dataset picker (`js/shell/datasets.js`: `{path: 'university', name: 'University', group: 'Living', at: true, en: true}`). The other demos carry 0.6.0 until they adopt 0.6.1; until then their pickers and the demo hub do not list University, and their shell bytes differ from this repository's in `datasets.js` and `manifest.json` only.
- Everything else in the shell is as School 0.6.0 describes it: the header and dataset picker, the address grammar (`?at=<production path>&e=<kind>:<key>` with `m`, `f.<filter>`, `focus`, `view`, `v3d`; this demo opens provinces, its `e` kinds are `site`, `uni`, `unit` and `station`, provisional until the registry declares them), the breadcrumb, the tab title within 60 characters, the launch card that sets the theme before the first paint, the partial-result notice (`PARTIAL` in `js/app.js`: every optional file has an entry and its failure calls `partial(key)`), the map budget by device, the lost-context restart, the phone sheet, and the map modules (camera, terrain, x-ray, building join, building units, MVT, map style, MapLibre guard, vendors).
- On citymeter.pages.dev the modules are also served from `/…/r/<digest>/` with a one-year cache: never give a module an address relative to the page, keep data fetches relative to the page (`data/…`). That site sends a Content-Security-Policy: a new outside host must be added there first.

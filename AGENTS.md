Hotel-only and School-only features (chain logos and room prices, district fills, the comparison set, learning services, closures) have no counterpart here and are not carried.

- Read `PRODUCT_STATEMENT.md` before changing what the first screen argues. The question is “นักศึกษากระจุกอยู่จังหวัดไหน และรอบแปลงมีวิทยาเขตแบบไหน”.
- Two grains, never mixed (`DATA_METHOD.md` §2). **Reporting units** (the MHESI student file 2568/2: a university, campus or institution the file reports separately) carry the students and the province the file names for them; they are the measure. **Sites** (the research team's reviewed university areas, `29_DEMO_SITES`) carry the points, the extents, the faculties and the context; a site shows its linked unit's figure as that unit's (“นักศึกษาตามหน่วยรายงาน …”), never as the students on that campus. A unit is linked to at most one site and a site names at most one unit; every link says how it was made (`js/model.js#LINKS`). A site without a link shows no student figure — never 0.
- Measure is a contract (`js/model.js#METRICS`): students reported (Σ students of the reporting units, “คน”, `li.supply`) by default, reporting units (“หน่วย”, `count`) on the switch; the map, legend, labels, Aha, ranks and charts read the same measure. Never call a figure an enrolment rate, demand, seats, capacity, a catchment, a market share, a density or a ranking of quality.
- Province is the finest area. Membership is the province the student file names for the unit; never re-assign it by a point or a name at runtime. The 77 production provinces are drawn; a province without a reporting unit is measured zero (outline, no fill) and is listed by name.
- Types are the eight groups of `meta.types` bound by stable id to LDS series with their shape cue (`js/cues.js#GROUP_SERIES`; no star). The two open-admission universities are the `open` type, by id, never by a name.
- Levels come from the file's level columns only: ป.ตรี · ป.โท · ป.เอก · อื่น ๆ (ปวช. ปวส. ประกาศนียบัตรบัณฑิต ประกาศนียบัตรบัณฑิตชั้นสูง ประกาศนียบัตร และอนุปริญญา). Never infer a level from a name.
- Aha first: on the whole country, the provinces with the most of the measure (at most three; never cut through a tie, never every province that holds the measure; `js/area-insight.js#topWithoutTie`), their share of the national total with numerator and denominator, the same share without the open-admission universities as a second bar (both bases in each bar's title and accessible text) and the provinces with no reporting unit by name; in a province, the universities with the most of the measure (at most three) and their share of the province. Snapshot numbers in the handoff JSON are fixtures, never UI constants.
- Fees are per programme as the university announces them (`32_DEMO_OFFERINGS` → `33_DEMO_FEE_LINKS`, the pilot campuses only): every line its own amount in its own currency (`js/model.js#money`, never converted), the research codes in words (`FEE_WORDS`, `cohortWords`; `tests/views.test.js` fails on a code without a word), the announcement linked. Never sum, average or compare fees across programmes or universities, never call one a price level or the cost of a degree, and never read a programme in an announcement as a programme admitting students now (the heading is “ค่าธรรมเนียมตามประกาศ”, the count “หลักสูตรในประกาศ”).
- Quality (`08_QUALITY`) is shown verbatim with its framework, dates and source, never as a rank or a score to compare.
- Logos come only from the research team's package (`LOGO_LIBRARY`), checked against its SHA-256 and shipped content-addressed (`assets/logos/<sha16>.<ext>`); they identify the university, under the name, on a plate of the surface the package names. Reuse terms are not established in the source: keep the note in `DATA_METHOD.md` and never use a logo as decoration.
- Campus extents are OpenStreetMap areas the research team matched (ODbL), approximate: “กรอบราว N ไร่” (m² / 1,600), never a deed, a legal boundary or a land size. An area the university publishes is shown as it is written (`officialArea`): a single figure as a chip, a longer note under the sources.
- Faculties of a campus: the located ones (`31_DEMO_UNIT_SITES`) under “คณะในพื้นที่นี้” with “N คนตามหน่วยรายงาน”; the linked unit's other academic units folded under “คณะอื่นตามหน่วยรายงาน · ยังไม่มีตำแหน่งบนแผนที่” with the student file's figure, never on the map (0.1.2; `js/profile-model.js#unlocatedOf`: by the index `uf`, a located faculty without an academic unit keeps only a row of exactly its name out, placeholders and campus labels are not faculties). Faculty locations are research data: a new one goes into the research master (as the Chula pilot of 2026-11-12 did, `DATA_METHOD.md` §6a) and comes back through the export — never patched in `data/`. Coordinates from Google Maps never enter the data.
- Faculty buildings: a faculty's building geometry joins the drawn buildings by its interior point (`pt`) at runtime; the building panel says “สูงราว” and heads its list “คณะในอาคารนี้” — the faculties whose geometry falls in the drawn outline, by name only (a building carries no student figure; 0.1.1), never “ตั้งอยู่ใน” beyond that. Only a faculty building opens the building view.

## Search — names found the way people type them (owner 2026-11-12)

- Since release 0.2.0 (citymeter-shell 0.7.0; owner 2026-11-12 12:05, VERBATIM: “ศึกษาแนวคิดเรื่อง Fuzzy search แล้ว implement กับ demo web ให้ได้ user benefit สูงสุดตาม user intent ในขณะนั้น และตาม product statement”; 13:15 “รวม university ไว้ด้วยกันเลย”) the search box lists names instead of filtering while it is typed in. `js/shell/fuzzy.js` is the matcher — the method of the CityMETER area-search brief (02-CityMETER__fuzzy-search-tech-brief v1, 19 Oct 2026, revised 20 Oct), moved from PostgreSQL to the browser because a demo is a static site: a name as typed, without its tone marks, by letters that sound alike, with its lead word (จ. อ. เขต) or the place it is in, typed on the other keyboard layout, or one or two keys off; the last three are guesses, made only when the sure steps find little. `js/shell/search.js` is the list under the box (a WAI-ARIA combobox in the flow of the panel) and `js/find.js` gives it this demo's names: provinces, institution types, universities, campuses, reporting units that have a name of their own, and rail stations (university, campus and unit names are Thai only in the data, so they are found and shown in Thai).
- Intent decides the action: a province opens its students and reporting units, an institution type its filter, a university its campuses and units, a campus its page on the map, a reporting unit its students, a station the campuses round it. Enter with nothing chosen applies the words as the text filter, as the box always did; when the words keep no row, Enter marks the first name found as typed and Enter again opens it — a row is never opened unmarked. The map and the panel do not change while the reader types.
- What is chosen is shown. A filter or words chosen from the list apply to the whole page: the place being looked at is left (the province stays — a province without the brand is a reading of that province) and the other filters that would leave the choice no row are lifted first, the fewest that do (`makeWay` → `filtersToLift`, js/shell/search.js). An item chosen always opens: the filters a university or a reporting unit does not pass — its province too — are lifted (`makeWayFor` → `filtersAgainst`), and a campus clears them itself (`selectSite`). The `filters` option of `installSearch` (js/app.js) names the filters that may be lifted — never a counting unit or a measure. The number on the list's last row is the rows the words keep with the filters that are on — or, when those leave them no row, with the ones that stay once the fewest are lifted, as choosing the row does (`rowsAfterWay`).
- A guess is never chosen for the reader (the brief's decision of 10 Oct 2026: “เลือกแถวให้อัตโนมัติเฉพาะเมื่อมีพื้นที่เดียวที่ชื่อตรง”). Only an entry that alone is written with the very name typed, or that name with its place, is marked ahead — never a university, a campus or a station, and never an entry that only goes by the name once its head word is left out (“ภูเก็ต” is the province; เทศบาลนครภูเก็ต is found by it and is a row below).
- A row says what an entry is and where, in words: no number and no caveat. How sure a match is stands in words too — the tag “สะกดต่างจากที่พิมพ์”, the heading “ชื่อใกล้เคียง”.
- Nothing typed is stored or sent: no query log and no feedback button (the brief keeps both for after its PDPA review).
- Other names of a place are the four of `PROVINCE_AKA` (js/shell/fuzzy.js) and a district's name without เมือง. Who keeps a fuller alias list is an open decision of the brief: do not grow the list by hand. A name the data carries in one language is found in that language only.
- A new kind of entry needs its line in `searchNote` (js/find.js), its action in `openFound` (js/app.js) and, on an English page, the words of its line in js/lang-en.js (the `.find-where` context); `tests/find.test.js` checks them. The demo's own `#search` input stays the field of the applied text filter (hidden, read by the code as before); if js/shell/search.js does not load, the box filters as it always did.
- On a phone, while a name is typed the panel stands tall and the box comes to its top, so the list has room above the keyboard (the list's `onFocus` in js/app.js sets this demo's sheet to its full height and its `pick` brings it back to its resting height once a name is chosen, so the map shows the choice; `html.find-typing` and the box's `scroll-margin-top` are in navigation.css).
- Before a release, type in the box at 393 px and on a desktop, in Thai and in English: a name as written, one without its tone marks, one with a wrong key, one on the other keyboard layout, a text that is no name — and, with a filter on, a name of another kind (the filter gives way). A word and the ways it is said are one word (`SEARCH_HEADS`: ม. and มหาลัย = มหาวิทยาลัย, มทร. = มหาวิทยาลัยเทคโนโลยีราชมงคล, มรภ. = มหาวิทยาลัยราชภัฏ), and a name that begins with one also goes by the rest: “เชียงใหม่” lists the province first — chosen ahead — and the universities that go by its name after it; “ธรรมศาสตร์” lists the university before the station of that name, and neither is chosen ahead. The short names people say (มช., มธ., จุฬาฯ) are not in the data and are not made up here.
